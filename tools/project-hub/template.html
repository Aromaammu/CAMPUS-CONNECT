Overview

The product

Our own live-class and messaging platform for the institute and its franchise branches. Head office controls every branch. Teachers run classes inside the app with automatic attendance, recordings, chat, polls and a whiteboard. Students see their courses, timetable, attendance, performance, fees and final project.

Build phases

How I work

  • Supervised: I finish one phase, test it, and stop with a "what to check" list for you.
  • Unsupervised: I go phase after phase on my own. Every card is tested (unit, server and headless browser tests plus a screen walkthrough) before it moves to Completed. I only pause for things only you can give.
  • Say "supervised" or "unsupervised" at any time to switch.
  • "Ready for test" means it is built and passes my tests, and now needs you (or something from you).

How work is saved

  • Two branches: development for all work, production for the live app.
  • A checkpoint is saved before every phase.
  • Production only changes by merging development after a review, when you say so.
